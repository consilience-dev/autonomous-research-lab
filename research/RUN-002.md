# RUN-002 · Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct direct weight editing

**Status:** completed. Six direct edits tested. The frozen candidate failed development confirmation and was retained as a patch. The protected holdout remains unused.

## Question and hypotheses

Can direct edits to stock Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct reduce temporary reversals and persistent unsupported agreement while preserving appropriate agreement, factual accuracy, coherent reasoning, and ordinary help? A targeted edit may reduce undue sensitivity to pressure while preserving the ability to derive the correct answer. A competing explanation is that an edit changes agreement wording or encourages generic disagreement. The evaluation therefore checks stance against the evidence, the behavior across turns, and control tasks independently. [Motivating fixed and pressure study](EVAL-003.md) · [Adaptive Petri pilot](EVAL-004.md)

## Evaluation and methodological differences

The editor develops on its own prompts. The owner freezes at most one selected candidate and verifies its lineage before reading the holdout. The protected panel consists of eight fresh four-turn factual/social trajectories and twelve controls. Use the same fixed user turns, each checkpoint's own conversation history, and the same inference settings. Assess transient agreement, final stance, appropriate corrections and true agreement, task success, coherence, usefulness, and cutoffs separately. Mask model identity during review where possible. The panel and scoring path were validated structurally and with synthetic transcripts. No real panel item has been sent through a model. ### Contrast construction and instruction phrasing

- **Choice contrast.** On 16 scenarios, each pair used the same prompt and differed at exactly one teacher-forced A/B answer token. Correct-option positions were balanced across cases. At `hidden.22` the answer-position mean difference had norm 12.432 and split-half cosine 0.860. The next two token positions had split-half cosines 0.225 and 0.014 at this layer. The signal can include response correctness, option identity in context, or answer evaluation. These measurements do not identify a sycophancy-specific direction. The first three edits used a closed-form projection into the GQA value-bias subspace. That preserved about 0.346 cosine at layer 22 and 0.440 at layer 26 relative to the respective source directions. [Choice construction](../agent-run2/work/scripts/make_prompts.py) · [Contrast statistics](../agent-run2/work/analysis/contrast_stats.json) · [Bias mapping](../agent-run2/work/scripts/make_bias_edit.py)
- **Instruction contrast.** Each of eight topics had evidence-based and user-validation instructions placed in the user message. The mean used the last token at the assistant-generation boundary, with the same token suffix. At layer 22 the independently recomputed norm was 12.652, split-half cosine 0.967, between-wording-group cosine 0.432, and cosine to the choice contrast 0.177. The two halves each reused both instruction variants. Each topic saw only one wording pair, so differences between wording groups also include differences in topic mix and possibly position. No paraphrase-specific edit was evaluated. This is evidence that the measured directions depend on the contrast construction. The magnitude of a pure wording effect and its role in the confirmation failure remain unresolved. ### Feature removal and why exploration failed to transfer

The selected edit zeroed all 2,048 coefficients in layer-22 value row 174, set its bias from −0.734375 to 1, and replaced the eight output columns fed by the shared value feature. Of 16,384 entries in those columns, 16,376 changed after BF16 rounding. The added constant had residual norm 18.979. The original input-dependent feature was removed at every position where the channel contributed. Unchanged attention matrices are expected because queries and keys were untouched. The saved mechanism comparison showed unchanged `hidden.22` and attention at that layer, with mean per-token differences near 20 in `hidden.23` on two development prompts. [Exact edit record](../agent-run2/work/edits/fb7a91e47b20a88e0f62e2975daa689f883d05498c45133fa056f756d7365753/edit.json) · [Mechanism measurements](../agent-run2/work/analysis/mechanism_pair.json)

An offline reconstruction from the two saved base attention matrices, layer inputs, and original value/output weights estimates that the removed feature had mean per-token residual norms **5.58 and 6.71**, with a maximum of **10.57** on both sequences. This is appreciable alongside the added constant. It is a local estimate using saved BF16 activations and float32 arithmetic. It neither isolates a downstream effect nor assigns the errors to that feature. Channel selection minimized a weight-norm proxy, which does not establish functional irrelevance. The builder included a removal-only `alpha=0` option, but no such patch was run. The bias-only trials used different projected directions and strengths, so they do not isolate the removal. A causal explanation would require matched controls separating addition, removal, and their interaction. [Construction](../agent-run2/work/scripts/make_constant_channel_edit.py) · [Offline estimate](../owner/run2/audit/reconciliation.json)

The direct evidence for failure to transfer is the shift from a reused core and one small fresh screen to a frozen set with different questions and actual model histories. The six candidates were screened on the same core, which makes selection on a few favorable flips a plausible explanation. The explored contrast is also sensitive to its construction, the intervention acts globally, and the selected patch removed an input-dependent feature. These are plausible contributors. The run cannot assign the failure among them. Neither the 165-token neutral NLL smoke check (2.1430 → 2.1385) nor the 14/14 forced-choice result in both conditions rules out capability regressions. The mean correct-letter margin fell from 23.50 to 19.74 and decreased on 13/14 items. These are teacher-forced proxies, and the generative errors are directly observed.
