## Findings and limitations

The audit found 82 observed model-entry records associated with counted lab calls, zero outside-lab entries, and no evidence of training or evaluation-data access in the observed commands and scripts. Hooks can miss uninstrumented activity. Agent 2's statement that two false claims were corrected includes the leap-year item, where the base already supplied the correct number alongside a false endorsement. Most candidates had only a small, reused pilot screen. The confirmation was agent-authored, used a qualitative rubric and one greedy output per prompt, and had no independent rater.
