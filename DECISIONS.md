# Methodological decisions

This is an append-only record of major choices. A later choice should cite and supersede the relevant decision ID, with its new evidence and date. Dates below are stated only where supported.

|---|---|---|

BF16 preserves the original parameter representation for exact patch replay. | A small stock model is a low-cost starting point for instrumentation and exploratory work. Code review and owner logs provide additional compliance evidence. The paper's separate forward service and 300-execution kernel limit were not reproduced.
